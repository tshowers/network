About NetworkA Taliferro Tech product · Part of TODD

Network turns contacts into a relationship system.

Network keeps every person, company, and conversation connected so TODD can surface who needs attention and what to do next — instead of relying on memory or a spreadsheet no one updates.

Help About

What it is

Every relationship gets a stage.

Network gives each contact a stage — Contacted, Engaged, Qualified, Opportunity, Customer — so it always knows where a relationship really stands, not just that it exists.

Category

Relationship intelligence, not a static CRM.

Network combines contact records with TODD Action Priorities, relationship-health scoring, and a pipeline view — built to answer "who should I talk to this week," not just store names and emails.

Who made it

Taliferro Tech, LLC.

Network is made by Taliferro Tech and is part of TODD, the Momentum System. Taliferro Tech creates connected software products that help people move from information to action.

You can't grow every relationship equally. Network shows you which ones deserve your attention.

Most contact tools are a place to store information. Network is built to be a place that tells you what to do with it — which relationships are stalling, which are warming up, and who to reach out to this week.

Stages, relationship-health meters, and TODD Action Priorities all exist for the same reason: to turn a list of names into a working network.
